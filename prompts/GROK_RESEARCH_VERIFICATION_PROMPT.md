# Grok Research and Verification Prompt — Nobu

Research current external facts for **Nobu**, a post-purchase price-monitoring platform whose first live retailer integration monitors eligible Target.com purchases for possible lower Target online prices during Target's price-adjustment window.

This is an external research audit, not a product brainstorming exercise.

## Mandatory official sources to verify first

- OKX.AI Genesis Hackathon page
- OKX A2MCP guide
- OKX ASP introduction and registration guide
- Target official Price Match Guarantee and related help pages
- SerpApi Google Shopping API documentation
- SerpApi pricing and legal documents

## Research tasks

1. Report any change to the hackathon deadline, listing requirement, demo rule, categories, judging criteria, or ASP technical requirements.
2. Report any change to Target's adjustment window, supported purchase types, proof requirements, exclusions, Target Plus rules, Alaska/Hawaii rules, or claim route.
3. Report any change to SerpApi's free quota, Google Shopping fields, caching, price-monitoring support, terms, or legal-shield availability.
4. Find current public user discussions about missing Target price drops, successful/failed adjustments, price verification friction, and existing price trackers.
5. Find current competing products, but distinguish automatic tracking from manual reminders and affiliate price-comparison tools.

## Evidence rules

- Official policy facts must come from official sources.
- Provide direct URLs, dates, and exact relevant wording or a faithful paraphrase.
- Separate official facts, provider claims, user reports, and your own inference.
- Do not treat Reddit/X complaints as policy.
- Do not invent engagement, product capabilities, API fields, or usage.
- Flag conflicts and stale sources.
- Do not propose a different retailer or change the product unless evidence shows the Target/SerpApi path is not viable.

## Output

A. Material changes since 2026-07-13  
B. Target policy diff  
C. SerpApi capability/terms diff  
D. User pain and competitor evidence  
E. Impacted source-of-truth files  
F. Recommendation: `NO CHANGE`, `UPDATE REQUIRED`, or `BLOCK BUILD`
