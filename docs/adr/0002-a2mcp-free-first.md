# ADR 0002 — Free A2MCP First

**Status:** Accepted  
**Date:** 2026-07-13

## Decision

Launch a free HTTP 200 A2MCP endpoint first. Do not let x402 payment work delay listing.

## Consequences

- Marketplace eligibility and service stability take priority.
- Paid monitoring or recovery packs are optional post-listing work.
- Any future x402 service must use the current official OKX Payment SDK/protocol and eligibility rules.
