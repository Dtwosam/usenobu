# Nobu FAQ

**Status:** ACTIVE  
**Aligned:** Lane 8R.2

### What does Nobu do?

Nobu monitors the exact product after purchase and alerts you when a safely matched lower price may create an opportunity to request the difference from the retailer.

### Why does Nobu monitor prices after purchase?

Retailer prices can drop after checkout. Monitoring helps you notice a possible difference while the adjustment window may still apply. Nobu does not guarantee that a lower price will appear.

### Does Nobu contact the retailer?

No. You contact the retailer. Nobu shows supporting information and the official contact path.

### Does Nobu guarantee a price adjustment?

No. The retailer verifies the price, checks eligibility, and makes the final decision.

### Which retailer is supported?

**Target is the only retailer currently supported.** Eligible Target.com and Target app purchases only. Target Plus is excluded.

### Will Nobu support more retailers?

**Additional retailers are planned, but each one must be separately integrated and verified before Nobu claims support.**

### What does the $0.99 payment cover?

It activates scheduled monitoring for **one confirmed and eligible purchase**. It does not guarantee a lower price, alert, refund, adjustment, or savings.

### Does payment guarantee an alert?

No. Payment only activates monitoring. Prices may stay the same, and matching fails closed when a lower price cannot be safely confirmed.

### Can Nobu be used through OKX.AI?

Yes. Compatible AI-agent environments can set up a purchase, confirm the product, activate monitoring, and manage monitors through conversation.

### Can Nobu be used through the website?

Yes. The UseNobu website supports visual purchase intake, product confirmation, alerts, and the Action Center.

### Which actions are free?

Discovery, product confirmation, email verification, preflight checks, status checks, listing monitors, and email-alert preference changes are free. Scheduled monitoring activation requires the one-time $0.99 payment.

### Where do observed prices come from?

Third-party SerpApi Google Shopping observations filtered toward Target. They are not an official Target API.

### Why does exact-product matching matter?

A lower price is only useful when it belongs to the same product and variant. Nobu fails closed when it cannot verify the match.

### Why is email verification required?

So consented alerts go to an address you control. Nobu does not use your Target password.

### Can email alerts be disabled?

Yes, anytime through the website (signed in) or OKX.AI monitor management.

### Can monitoring be stopped?

Yes. Stopping ends scheduled checks for that purchase. It does not submit a retailer request.

### Does Nobu require Target login information?

No. Nobu never collects Target passwords, card details, bank credentials, or 2FA codes, and does not access retailer accounts.
